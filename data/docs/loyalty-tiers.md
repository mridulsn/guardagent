# Loyalty tiers

Every customer belongs to one of three tiers: Bronze, Silver or Gold.

- Bronze: the default tier. No discount.
- Silver: reached after 500 USD of lifetime spend. 5% off every order.
- Gold: reached after 1500 USD of lifetime spend. 10% off every order and free return shipping.

The tier discount is applied to the item subtotal before tax and shipping.
Tier discounts do not stack with bulk discounts; the larger of the two applies.
Tiers are reviewed on the first day of each quarter.
