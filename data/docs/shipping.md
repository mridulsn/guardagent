# Shipping

Standard shipping takes 3 to 6 business days and costs 7.95 USD per order.
Orders over 120 USD ship free with standard shipping.
Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs 19.00 USD per order, for every tier.
We ship to the United States, Canada and the United Kingdom only.
Orders placed before 2 pm Eastern time on a business day leave the warehouse the same day.
An order moves from "processing" to "shipped" when the carrier scans the parcel.
