# Returns policy

Quillmoor Supply Co. is a fictional outdoor gear retailer used in this demo.

Unused items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.
Opened or used items can be returned within 14 days of delivery and are refunded as store credit, minus a 10% restocking fee.
Sale items marked "final sale" cannot be returned. Other sale items follow the normal 30 day window for unused items.
Return shipping is free for Gold tier members. Everyone else pays a flat 6.50 USD return label fee.
Refunds are issued within 5 business days after the warehouse receives the item.
