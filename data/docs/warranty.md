# Warranty

Tents, backpacks and sleeping bags carry a 2 year warranty against manufacturing defects.
Stoves and lanterns carry a 1 year warranty.
Apparel carries a 90 day warranty against seam and zipper failure.
The warranty does not cover wear and tear, misuse, or damage from open flame.
To make a claim, contact support with the order number and a photo of the defect.
